# Service contracts

A service contract describes what a service on one NixOS configuration runs as, offers and needs, so other modules can derive their configuration from it.

## Language

**Contract**:
A typed, writable description of one service's identity and claims within one configuration. It names no fleet concept, such as a network, except the node of a reference, and holds none of the service's own settings.
_Avoid_: Manifest, interface, surface

**Service**:
Something that runs on the configuration and that a contract describes. A helper daemon with its own process, such as a metrics exporter, is a service of its own.
_Avoid_: Integration, unit, workload

**Identity**:
What a service effectively runs as: a static account, or a dynamic identity that systemd allocates for one named unit. Who owns the service's files follows from it.

**Claim**:
A service's declared hold on one unit of something the configuration provides or needs: a bind, a mount, a secret or an inbox. Two services cannot hold the same claim.
_Avoid_: Resource, requirement, building block

**Claim kind**:
One kind of claim, such as binds, mounts, secrets or inboxes. The set of kinds is open: a new kind is defined the same way as the shipped ones.

**Location**:
The names that place a claim within its node: its service and id, or for a copy an inbox holds, the receiving service and inbox and the sent claim's node, service and id. A backend names its entry for a claim after it.
_Avoid_: Address, path, key

**Claim name**:
The name a claim goes by across the node collection, made of its node identity, service and id, such as `beta-influxdb_influxdb_data`. No two claims share one; a reference does not name a claim by it.
_Avoid_: Key, full name

**Claim set**:
The read-only, configuration-wide collection of one kind's claims from enabled contracts, each as its contract holds it, by service and id.

**Bind**:
Where a service accepts traffic: a protocol, a port and the addresses it listens on. It says where the service listens, never who may reach it.
_Avoid_: Listener, endpoint

**Mount**:
A path whose contents must survive, under one class. It says what must survive, never how a backend keeps it.
_Avoid_: Persisted path, bind mount, share

**Class**:
The name of a root of persistent storage a mount belongs to, such as state or cache. Where a class physically lives is up to an adapter.
_Avoid_: Category, tier

**Secret**:
A runtime credential a service needs. It says what the service reads, who may read it and where its value comes from, never how a backend stores, shares or installs it.
_Avoid_: Credential, age secret, key

**Secret value**:
Where a secret's value comes from: provided by a person or another party, generated by the service, copied from one other secret, or derived by a generator from the secrets it references.
_Avoid_: Provenance, source

**Copy**:
A secret whose value is that of one other secret it references. A service holds one it writes itself, and an inbox holds one for each secret it receives.
_Avoid_: Duplicate, replica

**Reference**:
A pointer to one claim by its node, kind, service and id. It always names its node, so it means the same claim on every node that reads it. A copied or derived secret's `sources` are the references its value comes from.
_Avoid_: Handle, link, dependency

**Inbox**:
A service's hold on a place where claims of one kind arrive from any node, its own included. It says what the service accepts, and for secrets, whether and how it holds a copy of each.
_Avoid_: Receiver, mailbox, channel

**Send**:
A claim's naming of an inbox it goes to. The sending node writes nothing on the receiving node.
_Avoid_: Contribution, offer, export

**Copy set**:
The read-only, configuration-wide collection of one kind's copies that the node's inboxes hold, by location, kept apart from the claim sets.

**Send check**:
An opt-in check that what each node sends does not depend on what its inboxes received.
_Avoid_: Routing check

**Node**:
One NixOS configuration among those evaluated together, whether it describes a host, a guest or an external device. A node writes its own contracts and overrides the service modules' defaults; a reference names a node by its node identity.
_Avoid_: Host, machine, peer

**Node collection**:
The nodes a reference may name, each by its node identity, as one configuration is given them.
_Avoid_: Nodes list, peers

**Node identity**:
A node's name within its node collection, independent of its hostname.
_Avoid_: Hostname, node name

**Generator**:
A named way to make a secret's value, such as `alnum` or `basic-auth`, alone or from the secrets it references. A backend adapter provides the generators a secret may name.
_Avoid_: Generator script, provenance, source

**Delivery**:
Handing a secret to a dynamic identity's unit, so its daemon reads it from where the unit receives it rather than where the backend installs it. A delivery adapter provides it.
_Avoid_: Injection, mounting

**Adapter**:
An opt-in module that turns one claim kind's claim set and copy set into one backend's configuration, such as preservation or agenix-rekey.
_Avoid_: Materialiser, consumer

**Adapter slot**:
The place where one adapter gives a claim kind the data it needs, such as the secret backend's location and generators. Only one adapter fills a slot, and a node writes none.
_Avoid_: Rule, hook, setting

**Check**:
A condition over the configuration's contracts that must hold, such as two claims not clashing. A failed check reports its message.
_Avoid_: Assertion, validation
